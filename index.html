Crystal Crates

Move every crate onto a glowing rune.

Level 1
Moves 0

Use the arrow keys to move the explorer. Push crates onto rune tiles to solve the puzzle. Crates cannot be pulled.

How to play

  1. Use arrow keys to move.
  2. Push a crate by walking into it.
  3. Every crate must rest on a glowing rune.
Arrange the crates to begin.
